FAQ

Common questions.

Do you take single orders or only bulk?

Both. We build anything from a single security cabin to bulk labour accommodation for industrial plants.

Why buy from the factory instead of a dealer?

There's no middleman markup, you talk directly to the people who build your cabin, and you can customise everything.

Can I visit the factory before ordering?

Yes. Visit our Chennai plants to see the materials and build quality for yourself.

Can I relocate the cabin later?

Yes. Our cabins are designed to be lifted and transported, and our team can handle the move for you.

Can the cabin match our branding?

Yes. We customise the interior, exterior and paint colours, and the panels are ready for your logo and signage.
